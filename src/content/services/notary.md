## Can this court help me?

A justice of the peace is a notary by virtue of the office, with limited powers, and only within the court's own territory.

The Justice of the Peace **can**:

- administer oaths and affirmations;
- notarize sworn statements, affidavits, and acknowledgments;
- notarize bills of sale, transfers, donations, chattel mortgages, and other papers that affect movable property or its title, including vehicle title papers filed with the Office of Motor Vehicles;
- make protests.

The Justice of the Peace **cannot**:

- notarize any paper that sells, transfers, or mortgages land or buildings;
- notarize or prepare a will or a prenuptial agreement;
- prepare inventories, appraisements, or partitions;
- write your document for you.

For those, see a commissioned notary or a lawyer.
